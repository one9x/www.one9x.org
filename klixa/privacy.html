Klixa — Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 12, 2026

Klixa is a shared camera app for group events. People create or join a group, take photos, and everyone in the group sees the album. This page explains what information Klixa collects, how it is used, and how to contact us.

This is a plain-English policy. It is written to be read, not to cover every legal contingency. If anything here is unclear, reach out at the email below and we will clarify.

What Klixa collects

We try to collect as little as possible. The following is the complete list:

  • Display name. When you register, you enter a name so other group members can see who uploaded which photo. This is stored on our server.
  • Photos you capture or import. Every photo you upload to a group is stored on our servers and shared with other members of the same group. Photos are stored at full resolution, plus a small thumbnail for faster loading.
  • Group membership. We store which groups you are a member of and when you joined them.
  • Device-generated identifiers. When you register, we assign your install a random identifier so the server can tell your device apart from other devices. This identifier is not tied to any personal information beyond your display name.

What Klixa does NOT collect

  • No email address.
  • No password (there is no password-based login).
  • No phone number.
  • No advertising ID.
  • No location data.
  • No contacts or address book data.
  • No analytics on how you use the app.
  • No third-party tracking SDKs.

How your data is used

Your data is used to make the app work. Nothing else. Specifically:

  • Your display name is shown to other members of the same group.
  • Your photos are shared with every other member of every group you are a member of, and only them.
  • Your group membership determines which photos you can see and who can see your uploads.

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with third parties for advertising. We do not use it to train machine learning models.

Where your data is stored

Photos and metadata are stored on servers we operate. We use industry-standard providers for the underlying infrastructure (compute and object storage). All data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections.

Who can see your photos

Photos you upload to a group are visible to every other member of that group. Group membership is controlled by a six-character join code, which you share privately with the people you want to invite.

Anyone with the code can join the group and see past and future photos in it. Keep the code private. Do not post it in public places unless you intend the group to be open.

Permissions Klixa asks for

  • Camera. To take photos from inside the app.
  • Photos and media. To let you import existing photos from your device gallery into a group, and to save a copy of photos you capture back to your gallery so you still have them if you uninstall Klixa.
  • Notifications. To show upload and download progress when the app is in the background, so Android does not kill your uploads prematurely.
  • Internet. To upload photos and sync with the group.

Klixa does not use the advertising ID, location services, contacts, microphone, or any other sensitive permission.

Deleting your data

You can remove individual photos from any album by long-pressing the photo in the grid and tapping Remove.

To delete your entire account and all the photos you have uploaded, send an email from your registered address to the contact below with the subject "Delete my account" and we will delete your record and any associated photos within seven days.

Photos that other members have already received and downloaded to their own devices are outside our control. Deleting your account removes the server copy and your group memberships, but it does not reach into other users' phones.

Children

Klixa is not designed for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has registered, contact us and we will delete the account.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Material changes (new data categories, new sharing practices) will be flagged in the app itself.

Contact

Questions, concerns, deletion requests, or anything else: contact@one9x.org

Klixa is currently in internal testing. Features and data handling may change before public release. This policy will be updated to reflect any such changes.
